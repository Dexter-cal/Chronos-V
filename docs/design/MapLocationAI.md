# Map & Location AI Design Document

## 1. Overview

The Map & Location AI is a foundational module in the ChronoVerse engine responsible for the procedural generation of the high-level game world map and the management of all its locations. This module creates the canvas upon which the rest of the game world is built. It provides the essential geographical and spatial context for all other AI modules, including the World Builder AI, the Narrative AI, and the Environment AI.

## 2. Core Responsibilities

### 2.1. High-Level World Layout Generation
- **Procedural Map Generation:** The AI will generate a high-level 2D map of the game world. This includes the generation of:
  - Continents and islands
  - Oceans, seas, and lakes
  - Major biomes (e.g., forests, deserts, mountains, swamps)
- **Techniques:** This can be achieved using various procedural generation techniques, such as Perlin noise, simplex noise, or diamond-square algorithm.

### 2.2. Point of Interest (POI) Generation and Management
- **Procedural Placement:** The AI will procedurally place key points of interest on the map, ensuring they are located in logical places (e.g., cities near water, dungeons in mountains).
- **Types of POIs:**
  - Cities, towns, and villages
  - Dungeons, caves, and ruins
  - Natural landmarks (e.g., waterfalls, volcanoes)
  - Quest-specific locations
- **Location Data:** Each POI will have associated data, such as its name, type, size, and a list of connected locations.

### 2.3. Pathfinding and Connectivity
- **Major Routes:** The AI will generate major roads and pathways connecting the POIs. This will help create a sense of a lived-in world and will be used by the NPC AI for long-distance travel.
- **Navigational Data:** The AI will provide high-level pathfinding data that can be used by other systems (e.g., to calculate travel times between locations).

### 2.4. Data Provider for other Systems
- **World Builder AI:** The Map & Location AI will provide the high-level map data as a blueprint for the World Builder AI to use when generating the detailed 3D environments.
- **Narrative AI:** The Narrative AI will query the Map & Location AI for suitable locations for quests (e.g., "find a ruined castle for the final boss fight").
- **Player-facing Map:** The AI will provide the necessary data for the Godot client to render a 2D map for the player in the UI.

## 3. Integration

- **Environment AI:** The Map & Location AI will work closely with the Environment AI. The biomes generated by the Map & Location AI will determine the weather patterns and environmental hazards generated by the Environment AI.
- **Rocco AI:** Rocco can issue high-level goals to the Map & Location AI, such as "generate a new continent".

## 4. Future Enhancements

- **Dynamic Map Evolution:** The map could evolve over time based on in-game events (e.g., a new volcano could form, a city could be destroyed).
- **More complex biome simulation:** A more detailed simulation of biomes, including factors like temperature, rainfall, and altitude.
